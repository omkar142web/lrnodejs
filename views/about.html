The Story Behind the Code

I don't just write code; I document the "Why" behind the "How."

My journey started with a simple question: How does this work? This curiosity turned into a passion for e-learning and knowledge architecture. I believe that the best way to master a language or a framework is to be able to explain it to someone else—or to your future self.

This website serves as my external brain. It's where I deconstruct complex systems, revise core computer science fundamentals, and share the resources that helped me bridge the gap from beginner to builder.
